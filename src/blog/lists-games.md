Here's a small list of some of my all time favourite video games:

1. Command and Conquer Generals: Zero Hours - In my opinion the best game in the whole C&C series. Really wanted Generals 2 to happen, but world is cruel.

![Command & Conquer Generals: Zero Hours](/images/lists-games/cnc-generals.jpg)

2. Age of Empires series - Well what a surprise, another classic RTS game in the list.

![Age of Empires 2](/images/lists-games/aoe2.jpg)

3. Grand Theft Auto Vice City & San Andreas - Putting both the games in one place as I honestly cannot decide which one I like more.

![GTA Vice City](/images/lists-games/gta-vc.jpg)

![GTA San Andreas](/images/lists-games/gta-sa.jpg)

4. Half Life - I never played the second part but HL is a masterpiece in itself.

![Half Life](/images/lists-games/hl1.jpg)

5. Hearts of Iron IV - A really good grand strategy game, not too complicated, not overly simple.

![Hearts of Iron IV](/images/lists-games/hoi4.jpg)

6. Recoil (1999) - Not really a great or popular game but it is the first video game I ever played on a PC.

![Recoil](/images/lists-games/recoil.jpg)


7. Minecraft - I play it occasionally when updates drop.

![Minecraft](/images/lists-games/minecraft.jpg)


8. OpenTTD - Finally an open-source game in the list. One of the best transport management game in my opinion

![OpenTTD](/images/lists-games/openttd.png)

9. Call of Duty - The original first game not the modern rip-offs

![Call of Duty](/images/lists-games/cod.webp)

10. Team Fortress 2 - Honestly I play it because my laptop can't run CS2 properly.

![Team Fortress 2](/images/lists-games/tf2.jpg)


I think from this list its pretty obvious I am crazy for Real Time Strategy games. I tend to try each new game released, recent ones being Beyond All Reason (BAR) and WarFront Demo.
