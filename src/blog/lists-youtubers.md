While I watch a lot of YouTubers across different domains, this list is mainly aimed at tech or programming YouTubers.

- [Javidx9](https://www.youtube.com/@javidx9) a.k.a [OneLoneCoder](https://github.com/OneLoneCoder) - The OG beginner's programming guide. The olcPixelGameEngine really formed my perception on what a code-only engine should look and feel like.
- [tony-btw](https://www.youtube.com/@tony-btw) - Makes good video on Linux.
- [Zeibytes](https://www.youtube.com/@Zeibytes) - The professional distro hopper.
- [Hayyoth](https://www.youtube.com/@hayyoth-org) - Recently exploring this new channel and loving the vibes.
